Sources

The four apps that interrupt you most, in one quiet place.

Switch each one on in the Islet popup. Press Try to see what its island looks like.

GitHub

Review requests, mentions, assignments and CI results. Open the pull request or mark it read from the island.

Privacy

Your messages stay in your browser.

Islet has no servers and no account. There's nowhere for your data to go.

  • GitHubYour token is saved in this browser and sent only to GitHub's API.
  • GmailRead with the Google session you're already signed in to. No password, no Google Cloud app.
  • Slack, WhatsAppRead from their open tabs on your computer. Nothing is copied anywhere else.
  • Screen sharingHide text shows who wrote, never what they wrote.
FAQ

Questions

Does it show over other apps, or only in Chrome?

Islands appear at the top of the tab you're looking at in Chrome. When Chrome isn't the app in front, Islet also sends a normal system notification so nothing slips past.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers that run Chrome extensions, such as Edge, Brave and Arc.

Do I need to keep Slack and WhatsApp open?

Yes. Islet reads their notifications from the open tab, so keep each one open somewhere. A pinned tab works well and stays out of the way.

What kind of GitHub token does it need?

A classic personal access token with the notifications scope. GitHub doesn't let fine-grained tokens read notifications yet.

Will it flood me with old notifications?

No. When you switch a source on, Islet shows one summary such as "4 unread on GitHub". After that, each new item gets its own island, and no more than three at a time.

Can I make it quieter?

Pick the Minimal look for a single line with a dot, turn the sound off, and shorten how long islands stay. For GitHub you can limit it to reviews, mentions and CI.

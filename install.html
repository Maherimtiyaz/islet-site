Install Islet in two minutes.

Islet is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, early users install it straight from this file. Updates are a quick re-download.

Download Islet islet-extension.zip · version 1.0.0 · for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc on a computer

Then follow these steps

  1. Unzip itOpen your Downloads folder, right-click islet-extension.zip and choose Extract All… → Extract.
  2. Open Chrome's extensions pageType chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. Turn on Developer modeUse the switch in the top-right corner.
  4. Load itClick Load unpacked, open the extracted islet-extension folder, then click Select Folder. Pick the folder that has manifest.json directly inside it.
  5. Pin itClick the 🧩 puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the 📌 next to Islet. Open it any time with the icon or Alt+Shift+I.
  1. Unzip itOpen Downloads in Finder and double-click islet-extension.zip. A folder appears next to it.
  2. Open Chrome's extensions pageType chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Return.
  3. Turn on Developer modeUse the switch in the top-right corner.
  4. Load itClick Load unpacked, click once on the islet-extension folder, then click Select.
  5. Pin itClick the 🧩 puzzle piece, then the 📌 next to Islet. Open it any time with the icon or ⌥ Option+⇧ Shift+I.

You'll know it worked

A welcome page opens by itself, and your first island drops in at the top. Click the Islet icon, go to Send and press Pop it now, then switch on Gmail in Sources.

About the warning

While Islet is installed this way, Chrome may show a "developer mode extensions" notice. That's normal for early-access installs and goes away once you switch to the Chrome Web Store version.

On a phone or tablet?

Chrome on phones and tablets doesn't run extensions. Open this page on your computer.
